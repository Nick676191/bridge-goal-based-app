Common questions

I forgot my password

On the login screen, tap Forgot password?. We'll email you a code; enter it with a new password and you're back in.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Profile > Settings > Delete account, or email info@pynpro.net from the address on your account. Deleting removes your profile, goals, photos and messages. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I report a post or block someone?

Tap the … on a post, message or profile and choose Report or Block. We review reports, generally within 24 hours. For anything urgent, email us too.

Who can see my goals?

Everyone on PYN. There are no private accounts, so every goal has people behind it. Direct messages are only between you and the people in the conversation.

When can I get PYN?

PYN is in testing now and coming to the App Store. To join the beta, email info@pynpro.net with your name and the sport you played.
