How it works

  1. Post your goal

    Say what you're going after and your plan to start. PYN checks it against the SMART principles as you type. Add photos or a short video.

  2. People commit to it

    Friends can commit to your goal and post updates beside you, encourage it with a star, or reply with tips from their own training.

  3. Update until it's done

    Log progress as you go. Tag a setback when one comes; it shows as clearly as a win. Mark the goal achieved and everyone who committed hears about it.
